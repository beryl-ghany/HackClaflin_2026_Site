Something sweet is brewing.

HackClaflin 2027 is a student hackathon planned at Claflin University. We're bringing together students from Claflin and other schools to form teams, explore real problems, build creative projects, and share their work.

New to hackathons? You're welcome here. We're planning workshops and opportunities to learn from mentors. The candy factory theme brings a little imagination to the experience.
